Email header analyzer

Paste the raw headers of an email (or the whole raw message) and see the route it actually took: every relay hop with per-hop delays, SPF / DKIM / DMARC verdicts, and the classic spoofing red flags. Headers carry IP addresses, account names, and server names — exactly the things not to paste into a server-side analyzer. Here, nothing you paste leaves this page.

In most mail clients: “Show original”, “View source”, or “View raw message”. A body after the blank line is ignored — it never leaves your browser either way.
