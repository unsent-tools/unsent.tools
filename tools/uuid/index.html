UUID / ULID inspector

Paste a UUID or ULID and see what it actually carries: version, variant, and — for time-based kinds — the embedded creation timestamp, clock sequence, and node/MAC address. Identifiers from production systems are sensitive; here, nothing you paste leaves this page.

Accepts canonical UUIDs, {braced}, urn:uuid:…, bare 32-hex, and 26-char ULIDs.
