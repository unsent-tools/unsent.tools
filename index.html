unsent.tools small, correct, private web tools

unsent.tools

A growing set of single-purpose tools that run entirely in your browser. The name is the promise: nothing you type is sent anywhere — no backend, no network requests, no analytics, no ads. Each tool's logic is covered by tests before it ships.

  • Subnet / CIDR calculator
    Network, host range, mask, and count for any IPv4 or IPv6 CIDR block, with well-known-range classification.
  • JWT decoder
    Decode a JSON Web Token, explain its claims and expiry, and verify HMAC signatures — without pasting it into someone's server.
  • URL inspector
    Break a URL into its parts, decode percent-encoding and punycode hostnames, and spot embedded credentials before you share it.
  • Timestamp converter
    Epoch seconds/ms/µs/ns and ISO 8601, converted both ways — in UTC, your local timezone, and relative to now.
  • Text diff
    Compare two texts line by line with character-level highlights and unified diff export — without uploading either version anywhere.
  • Cron expression explainer
    Plain-English reading of any cron schedule, plus its next run times in your timezone or UTC.
  • chmod calculator
    Unix permissions as octal, symbolic, checkboxes and plain English — plus chmod expressions like u+x, verified against GNU chmod.
  • Color converter
    Hex, rgb, hsl, hwb, oklch and oklab, converted both ways — with WCAG contrast ratios and sRGB gamut checks.
  • UUID / ULID inspector
    Decode any UUID or ULID — version, variant, embedded timestamps, MAC addresses — and generate v4, v7, or ULIDs locally.
  • Number base converter
    Binary, octal, decimal, hex and any base 2–36 at arbitrary precision — with two's-complement views, byte order, and bit properties.
  • Hash calculator
    CRC-32, MD5, SHA-1/256/384/512 and HMAC of text or files, hex or Base64 — with checksum comparison, all in your browser.
  • Certificate decoder
    X.509 / TLS certificates decoded in your browser: subject, issuer, validity, SANs, key type, extensions, fingerprints — chains welcome.
Everything here is open source, seriously tested, and runs offline once loaded. Built and maintained by an autonomous AI agent.
